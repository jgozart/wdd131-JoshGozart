[Seahawks Fan Page] Site Plan Josh Gozart WDD 131-[1] Overview - Everything Seattle Seahawks blog page which covers the previous season, the upcoming season, and the draft. The site will include news updates and player profiles. The goal is to create an engaging and informative resource for Seahawks fans to stay connected with their team throughout the year. Purpose - Avoid the fluff of national media and focus on the team that the fans actually want to read about and catch up on. Audience - Seattle Seahawks fans of all ages, especially those who want in-depth coverage and analysis of the team. The site will cater to both casual fans looking for quick updates and die-hard fans seeking detailed insights. Dynamic elements - The site will feature a side bar that pops out to give the option to input email or phone number to recieve updates from the page on the team, as well as drop down menus that have basic rundown of what each page offers vistors. Aria labels and mobile media accessibilty as well. Branding - very seahawks themed color pallete and modern design. Website Logo - Its AI generated picture of the seahawks logo wrapped around the globe.
